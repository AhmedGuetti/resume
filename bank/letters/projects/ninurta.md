## story-ninurta
kind: story
tags: [agents, llm, automation, side-project, python, reliability, cost, product]
by: claude
en: >-
  Outside work, I built Ninurta, the tool that tailored this application. It reads a job
  offer, extracts its facts, and picks the most relevant content from a bank of everything I
  have done, in English and French. I designed it so the model chooses but never writes:
  every fact it reads from an offer must quote the offer, and any reference it makes up is
  dropped. It keeps a budget, falls back to a second model when the first answers badly, and
  costs about a tenth of a cent per application.
