## story-gmath
kind: story
tags: [c++, ml, algorithms, data-structures, computer-graphics, performance, side-project]
by: claude
en: >-
  On my own time, I am writing machine learning algorithms from scratch in C++, starting
  with 1-NN and k-NN. They sit on top of gmath, a static math library of my own with
  distance functions and a generic KD-tree. The same tree serves both nearest-neighbour
  search and rendering, so one data structure does two jobs. I wanted to understand what
  libraries like scikit-learn do under the hood, and C++ makes me think about memory and
  speed at every step. When I use those libraries at work now, I know what they cost.
fr: >-
  Sur mon temps libre, j'implémente des algorithmes de machine learning from scratch en C++,
  en commençant par 1-NN et k-NN. Ils reposent sur gmath, ma propre bibliothèque

  données pour deux usages. Je voulais comprendre ce que font des bibliothèques comme
  scikit-learn sous le capot, et le C++ m'oblige à penser à la mémoire et à la vitesse à
